# DEEPTHA NATURALS - MASTER DEVELOPMENT PLAN

## Phase Overview

- **Phase 1: Foundation & Docs** (Architecture, DB Schemas, Open Questions, Readme)
- **Phase 2: Database Models & Catalogue Seeding** (Full catalogue data from PDF with exact prices, weights, categories, nutrition facts, and podi/pickle/ghee/honey/oil variants)
- **Phase 3: Light Luxury Design System & Layout** (Header, Announcement Bar, Footer, Brand Story, Color Tokens, Mobile Nav)
- **Phase 4: Public Storefront** (Home, Shop, Category Filter, Product Detail Page, Variants, Ingredients, Nutrition, Reviews, Recipes & Blog)
- **Phase 5: Cart, Checkout & Payment Gateway** (Guest & Customer Checkout, Razorpay/Mock Payment Modal, Order Confirmation)
- **Phase 6: Customer Accounts & Orders** (Profile, Addresses, Order History, Timeline, Wishlist, Loyalty Points, Subscriptions)
- **Phase 7: AI Shopping Assistant & RAG** (Floating desktop / mobile modal chat with Gemini 3.6 Flash, grounded on catalogue knowledge)
- **Phase 8: AI Voice Calling Agent & Browser Tester** (Realtime voice test with waveform UI, speech synthesis, multilingual support for EN/TE/HI, tool calling: product search, cart add, order status lookup, lead creation, human handoff)
- **Phase 9: CRM & Customer 360° Console** (Lead Management, Customer Lifecycle stages, Voice call histories, Transcripts, AI Call summaries, CRM Timeline)
- **Phase 10: WhatsApp & Email Automation Engine** (Event-driven workflows for abandoned cart recovery, order updates, reorder reminders, win-back campaigns)
- **Phase 11: Admin Control Panel** (Product Management, Variant Stock, Orders, Customer 360 viewer, Voice Call Log dashboard, Campaign builder, Audit logs, RBAC)
- **Phase 12: SEO, Analytics & AI Business Insights** (Schema.org markup, revenue metrics, conversion funnel, AI inventory/sales insights)
- **Phase 13: Final Verification & Test Suites** (API tests, Voice scenario tests, Cart checkout flow validation, Build check)
